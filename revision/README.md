## How to revise (15 min per topic)
1. Open **Topic Cards**, turn on **Recall mode**, pick a topic.
2. Say the **What → Why → How → Solves** out loud, then reveal and compare.
3. Missed a part? Open the cheat sheet, then answer that topic's interview questions out loud.
4. Still shaky? Add it to the weak spots in CONTEXT.md and quiz it again in 3 days.

**Rule:** every new topic gets a card (What · Why · How · Solves + a memory hook + the interview line).
